# GoreeCloud Home — Branding

## Canonical identity

- Full product name: **GoreeCloud Home**
- Short installed/launcher name: **Home**
- Master brand: GoreeCloud
- Brand relationship: prefixed GoreeCloud family product
- GoreeCloud Suite member: yes
- Repository: `GoreeCloud/goreecloud-home`
- Package/application suffix: `home`
- Intended application identifier: `com.goreecloud.home`

Use **GoreeCloud Home** in formal product documentation, release materials, About/provenance surfaces and standalone contexts where ownership should be explicit. Use **Home** in launchers, installed-app lists, compact navigation and ordinary in-product contexts where the product relationship is already clear.

## Visual identity

No final product-specific icon or logo is declared by this repository foundation. Future visual identity must follow the current GoreeCloud application/service visual identity requirements and Glaze UI design language. Placeholder artwork must not be represented as canonical.

## Prohibited presentation

- Do not represent Home Assistant, Google Home or Apple Home/HomeKit branding as GoreeCloud Home branding.
- Do not reuse third-party product icons as the Home application icon.
- Do not claim Glaze UI conformance until application-specific evidence exists.
- Do not shorten the formal canonical identity to an ambiguous unapproved brand such as `GC Home`.

The repository-local branding record summarizes product identity; authoritative GoreeCloud branding standards continue to govern.
